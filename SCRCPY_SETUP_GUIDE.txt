================================================================================
     ACTIVKLASS MOBILE - SCRCPY SCREEN MIRRORING & APK SETUP GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide explains how to mirror your real Android phone's screen directly to 
your laptop during presentations, tests, and capstone defenses using scrcpy.

WHY SCRCPY?
- 100% Free & Open Source (No watermarks, no ads, no 5-minute time limits).
- Ultra-low latency (60 FPS, zero lag via USB cable).
- Does NOT require Android Studio or Expo running on your laptop.
- Uses almost 0% CPU/RAM on your laptop.
- You can control the phone using your mouse and keyboard or touch the phone directly!

================================================================================
PART 1: DOWNLOAD & EXTRACT SCRCPY ON YOUR LAPTOP
================================================================================

1. Download the scrcpy Windows package:
   - Go to: https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy/releases
   - Under the latest release (Assets section), download:
     "scrcpy-win64-vX.X.zip" (e.g., scrcpy-win64-v3.1.zip)

2. Extract the ZIP file:
   - Right-click the downloaded zip file -> "Extract All...".
   - Extract it to an easy-to-find folder, for example:
     C:\scrcpy   or on your Desktop: Desktop\scrcpy

3. Keep this folder open. The file you will run later is "scrcpy.exe".


================================================================================
PART 2: ENABLE USB DEBUGGING ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE
================================================================================

scrcpy needs "USB Debugging" turned ON to display your phone screen. You only 
have to do this once!

Step 1: Turn on Developer Options
- Open your phone's Settings.
- Scroll down and tap "About Phone" (or "About Device").
- Find "Build Number":
  * Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO: Tap "OS Version" or "MIUI version" 7 times.
  * Samsung: Tap "Software Information" -> tap "Build number" 7 times.
  * Oppo / Realme / Vivo: Tap "Version" -> tap "Build number" 7 times.
  * Stock Android / Pixel / Motorola: Tap "Build number" 7 times.
- You will see a toast notification: "You are now a developer!".

Step 2: Enable USB Debugging
- Go back to Settings.
- Search for "Developer options" (usually under System or Additional Settings).
- Scroll down to the "Debugging" section.
- Toggle ON "USB debugging".
  * NOTE FOR XIAOMI / POCO / REDMI USERS:
    Also enable "USB debugging (Security settings)" if you want to click or 
    type on the phone from your laptop using your mouse.


================================================================================
PART 3: CONNECT PHONE & START MIRRORING
================================================================================

1. Connect your phone to your laptop using a USB data cable.
   (Make sure the cable supports data transfer, not charging only).

2. Look at your phone screen!
   A popup will appear asking: "Allow USB debugging?".
   - Check the box: [x] "Always allow from this computer".
   - Tap "Allow" (or "OK").

3. On your laptop, open your scrcpy folder and double-click:
   ==> scrcpy.exe

4. SUCCESS!
   A clean, crisp window displaying your phone's screen will open immediately.
   Whatever you do on your phone will reflect on the laptop screen in real time.


================================================================================
PART 4: INSTALL THE ACTIVKLASS APK ON YOUR PHONE
================================================================================

You need the standalone ActivKlass APK installed on your phone (no Expo Go).

Method A (Fastest via scrcpy):
1. Start scrcpy so your phone screen is visible on your laptop.
2. Drag and drop the "ActivKlass.apk" file directly into the scrcpy window!
3. scrcpy will automatically push and install the APK onto your phone.

Method B (Manual phone install):
1. Copy the "ActivKlass.apk" file to your phone (via USB, Google Drive, or Discord).
2. On your phone, open your File Manager or Downloads folder.
3. Tap the APK file and select "Install".
   (If prompted, allow "Install from unknown sources").


================================================================================
PART 5: PRESENTATION PRO-TIPS FOR DEFENSE DAY
================================================================================

1. Turn ON "Do Not Disturb" on your phone:
   - Put your phone on DND / Silent mode before the defense so incoming calls, 
     Messenger chats, or personal notifications do not pop up on the projector!

2. Side-by-Side Screen Layout:
   - Position the scrcpy phone window on the right half of your laptop screen.
   - Position the Web Browser (Teacher/Admin portal) on the left half.
   - This lets your panel see real-time cross-platform synchronization 
     (e.g., teacher posts a quiz on web -> notification appears on mobile).

3. Keep Phone Screen Awake:
   - You can run scrcpy with the command:
     scrcpy --stay-awake
   - This prevents your phone from locking its screen while plugged into the laptop.

4. Useful Keyboard Shortcuts in scrcpy:
   - Right-click mouse:   Back button (Android Back)
   - Middle-click mouse:  Home button
   - Ctrl + F:            Toggle Fullscreen
   - Ctrl + H:            Press Home
   - Ctrl + S:            Switch App (Recent apps)
   - Ctrl + Up / Down:    Adjust volume


================================================================================
PART 6: TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Issue: Double-clicking scrcpy.exe opens and closes immediately.
Fix:
1. Make sure your phone is plugged in with USB debugging turned ON.
2. Check your phone screen to see if the "Allow USB debugging?" dialog is 
   waiting for your approval.
3. Try a different USB cable or a different USB port on your laptop.

Issue: scrcpy says "device unauthorized".
Fix:
Unplug the phone, plug it back in, unlock your phone screen, and tap "Allow" 
on the USB debugging popup.

Issue: Mouse clicks don't work on the mirrored screen (Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO).
Fix:
In Developer Options on your phone, enable "USB debugging (Security settings)" 
(requires logging into your Mi account). Alternatively, you can simply tap the 
physical phone screen directly during the presentation.
