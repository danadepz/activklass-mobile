================================================================================
          ACTIVKLASS - TESTER GUIDE: STANDALONE APK & SCREEN MIRRORING
================================================================================

This guide is for testers and presenters running the ActivKlass Mobile application 
on a physical Android phone and mirroring it to a laptop.

NO ANDROID STUDIO, EMULATORS, OR EXPO GO REQUIRED!
The mobile app runs directly on your phone as a standalone APK, connecting to our 
live Firebase cloud services.

================================================================================
PART 1: DOWNLOAD THE ACTIVKLASS APK
================================================================================

Download the latest release APK directly from our GitHub Releases page:

Direct APK Download Link:
https://github.com/danadepz/activklass-mobile/releases/download/v1.0.0-demo/ActivKlass.apk

Releases Page:
https://github.com/danadepz/activklass-mobile/releases/tag/v1.0.0-demo

Save "ActivKlass.apk" on your laptop (e.g. in your Downloads or Desktop folder).


================================================================================
PART 2: DOWNLOAD & SETUP SCRCPY ON YOUR LAPTOP
================================================================================

scrcpy mirrors your real phone screen to your laptop in smooth 60 FPS with 
almost zero laptop CPU usage and zero lag.

1. Download scrcpy for Windows:
   - Go to: https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy/releases
   - Under the latest release (Assets section), download:
     "scrcpy-win64-vX.X.zip" (e.g., scrcpy-win64-v3.1.zip)

2. Extract the ZIP file:
   - Right-click the downloaded zip file -> "Extract All...".
   - Extract it to an easy-to-find folder, for example:
     C:\scrcpy   or   Desktop\scrcpy

3. Keep this folder open. The file you will run later is "scrcpy.exe".


================================================================================
PART 3: ENABLE USB DEBUGGING ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE
================================================================================

You only need to do this once on your phone!

Step A: Turn on Developer Options
- Open your phone's Settings.
- Tap "About Phone" (or "About Device").
- Find "Build Number" (or "OS/MIUI Version"):
  * Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO: Tap "OS Version" or "MIUI version" 7 times.
  * Samsung: Tap "Software Information" -> tap "Build number" 7 times.
  * Oppo / Realme / Vivo: Tap "Version" -> tap "Build number" 7 times.
  * Google Pixel / Motorola: Tap "Build number" 7 times.
- You will see a popup: "You are now a developer!".

Step B: Enable USB Debugging
- Go back to Settings -> search for "Developer options" (under System or Additional Settings).
- Scroll down to the "Debugging" section.
- Toggle ON "USB debugging".
  * NOTE FOR XIAOMI / POCO / REDMI:
    Also toggle ON "USB debugging (Security settings)" if you want to click or 
    type on the phone using your laptop mouse/keyboard.


================================================================================
PART 4: CONNECT PHONE & START MIRRORING
================================================================================

1. Plug your phone into your laptop using a USB data cable.
2. Look at your phone screen!
   A prompt will appear: "Allow USB debugging?".
   - Check the box: [x] "Always allow from this computer".
   - Tap "Allow" (or "OK").
3. On your laptop, open your scrcpy folder and double-click:
   ==> scrcpy.exe
4. Your phone's screen will instantly open in a clean, high-resolution window!


================================================================================
PART 5: INSTALL ACTIVKLASS ON YOUR PHONE (DRAG & DROP)
================================================================================

With scrcpy open and displaying your phone screen:
1. Locate the downloaded "ActivKlass.apk" on your laptop.
2. Click and DRAG the "ActivKlass.apk" file directly into the scrcpy window.
3. scrcpy will automatically install the app onto your phone!
4. The ActivKlass app icon will appear on your phone's home screen or app drawer.
5. Tap the app icon to open it.
